Slide 2 of 10 • Problem Statement

The Impersonation & Digital-Arrest Crisis

Across the nation, sophisticated fraud syndicates exploit citizen trust and administrative fear:

The Scam Anatomy

  • Fabricated Authority: Scammers pose as police, CBI, or tax officers on video calls using forged badges and uniforms.
  • Psychological Terror: Victims are placed under "digital arrest" for alleged money laundering or courier contraband.
  • Extortion Demands: Immediate orders to transfer life savings to personal UPI accounts or reveal one-time passwords (OTPs).

The Root Vulnerability

  • Blind Trust: Visual ID cards and badges are trivially photoshopped or deepfaked.
  • No Real-Time Verification: Citizens have no instant tool to verify whether a badge is genuine.
  • Policy Ignorance: Victims do not know that real government officers are strictly prohibited from demanding personal UPI transfers.

Slide 5 of 10 • Cryptographic Core

DID, Ed25519 & Verifiable Credentials

TrustGov uses open W3C cryptographic standards rather than proprietary identity silos:

Decentralized Identifiers (DID)

  • Format: did:trustgov:<uuid>
  • Public keys exported in multibase Ed25519 format (z6M...).
  • Zero Key Leakage: Private signing keys are never stored in database tables or exposed in API payloads.
  • Public DID Document resolution via GET /api/did/:did.

W3C Verifiable Credentials & Anti-Replay

  • Standard W3C VC schema signed by the TrustGov Issuance Authority DID.
  • Tamper Detection: Modifying a single character invalidates the Ed25519 signature math.
  • Proof of Possession: Dynamic 32-byte CSPRNG nonces with 5-minute strict TTL.
  • Single-Use State: Replaying consumed challenges returns HTTP 409 Conflict.
Slide 6 of 10 • Blockchain Anchoring

Blockchain Registry & Immutability

Smart contract registry (TrustGovRegistry.sol) anchored at 0x5FbDB2315678afecb367f032d93F642f64180aa3:

On-Chain Integrity Guarantees

  • Tamper Evidence: Off-chain database modifications cause an instant hash mismatch against on-chain SHA-256 digests.
  • Zero PII on Ledger: Only cryptographic hashes and status enums are recorded on-chain, protecting citizen privacy and keeping gas minimal.
  • Pure Read Verifications: Citizen queries do not invoke gas-consuming transactions.

One-Way Revocation Guardrails

  • ACTIVE ➔ SUSPENDED ➔ ACTIVE permitted for administrative duty leaves.
  • ACTIVE ➔ REVOKED or SUSPENDED ➔ REVOKED permitted for disciplinary actions.
  • REVOKED ➔ ACTIVE strictly rejected: Reversible activation of revoked officers is mathematically prohibited in smart contract bytecode.
Slide 7 of 10 • The Core Breakthrough

Separating Identity from Authorization

Security Maxim: Authenticity does not grant unrestricted authority.

Evaluation Tier Question Evaluated System Mechanism Outcome
Identity Verification "Is this person an authentic officer?" DID & Ed25519 signature proof VERIFIED
Action Authorization "Is this specific demand lawful and safe?" Server-Side Risk Policy Engine DENY (CRITICAL)

Mandatory Server-Side Hard Denials

  • REQUEST_UPI_PAYMENT, REQUEST_BANK_TRANSFER ➔ DENY (CRITICAL RISK)
  • REQUEST_OTP, REQUEST_PIN, REQUEST_REMOTE_ACCESS ➔ DENY (CRITICAL RISK)
  • Zero Client Trust: Backend strips and overrides any client-supplied decision values.
